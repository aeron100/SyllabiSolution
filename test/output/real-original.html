For reference, textbook information and technology requirements are in the  Course Materials, Software, & Technology Requirements page. This course uses the free eBook, "Open Data Structures"

The modules may contain linked content. To avoid losing your place on the current page in the Canvas course, right-click and select "Open in a New Tab."

Overview

Before we dive into the course content, you will complete the Course Orientation to familiarize yourself with the overall structure, expectations, and tools we will use. This week, we will also set up Eclipse as your Java development environment and help you become familiar with Java programming. You will write your first Java programs and get comfortable with Eclipse. Then, we will explore the foundations of data structures, discussing the distinction between a data structure's interface and its implementation and why this separation is essential. We will also cover critical mathematical concepts like asymptotic notation and probability, which are vital to analyzing algorithm efficiency.

M01 - Discussion: Introductions

Discussion Topic

Part 1

In this course, we’ll explore the fascinating world of data structures and how they are essential for efficient programming. Start by introducing yourself to your classmates. Share your academic background, your experience with programming or computer science, and what led you to take this "Data Structures with Java" course. What do you hope to gain from learning about data structures, and how do you see this knowledge benefiting your future career or projects?

Part 2

When you hear the term "data structures," what comes to mind? You might think of how lists, stacks, queues, or trees help organize and process information. Have you ever encountered a real-world problem where the organization of data played a crucial role? Feel free to share an experience or a story where data structures (or a lack of them!) impacted the outcome. If you’ve read an interesting article or have a relevant link, share it with the group!
